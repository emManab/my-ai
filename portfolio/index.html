The honest story

Not a press release. What AI actually helped with, and where I still had to make the call.

AI sped up the first draft. I still had to decide what was worth shipping.

During this build, I used AI as a pair programmer and writing partner: scaffolding UI, suggesting implementation paths, explaining errors, and tightening the copy. That saved time on repetitive work and let me spend more time on product decisions.
